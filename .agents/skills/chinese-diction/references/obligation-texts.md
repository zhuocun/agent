# Obligation texts and procedures

**Deontic force:** where the settled genre is one that imposes obligation — contract, policy, safety notice, regulatory text, but not a procedure such as instructions, a runbook or a recipe, whose required steps take `需` while a safety warning inside it stays a safety notice — the categorical word *is* the correct form and softening it is a defect. Contract, policy and safety Chinese carries `应` / `不得` / `严禁`, not `需` / `通常`.

**Modifiers in contracts:** **中文修饰与连接** in `SKILL.md` decides them as it does any other modifier.

- **Deontic force:** ✓ 严禁在设备通电时拆卸外壳 · ✓ 未经甲方书面同意，乙方不得转让本协议项下的权利和义务
- **Procedure step:** ✗ 一旦接近关口，就必须切换到人工 → ✓ 接近关口时，需要切换到人工
- **Absolute fact:** ✓ 盖章后不可逆 *(a fact; hedging it would be false)*
- **Scope-bearing contract phrase:** ✓ 本协议项下由甲方提供的、经乙方书面确认的技术资料
- **Packed modifier:** ✗ 由甲方在本协议签署之日起三十日内以书面形式提出的解除通知 → ✓ 该解除通知由甲方在本协议签署之日起三十日内以书面形式提出

## Checks

- [ ] Contract, policy, safety and regulatory text uses `应` / `不得` / `严禁` where it imposes obligation; required procedure steps take `需`; a safety warning inside a procedure stays a safety notice.
