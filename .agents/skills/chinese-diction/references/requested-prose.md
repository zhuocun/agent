# Requested prose

**Writing exception:** Requested prose — articles, stories, copy and other artifacts — follows its intended genre and purpose, including prose delivered inline. Use a figure only when it adds precision or an effect the piece needs; cut empty ornament. In persuasive writing, make claims the reader can check. A plain idea dressed as a coined or branded label, a slogan and hyperbole are empty ornament.

- **Suspense fiction:** ✓ 走廊尽头的灯像一只睁着的眼，盯着我 *(the simile and personification convey the narrator's feeling of being watched)*
- **Persuasive genres:** ✓ 十分钟就能上手，无需通读文档 over ✗ 开启高效协作新体验
- **Branded label:** ✗ 新基座的原语 → ✓ 新系统的基本构件
- **Slogan / hyperbole:** ✗ 回滚到最后一毫秒 → ✓ 操作可回滚到执行前的状态

## Checks

- [ ] Each figure adds precision or an effect the piece needs; no empty ornament, branded label, slogan or hyperbole; persuasive claims are checkable.
