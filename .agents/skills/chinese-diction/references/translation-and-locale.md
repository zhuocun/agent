# Translation, locale and script

**Translating:** read for *meaning*, then write that meaning as Chinese; never go clause-by-clause. Translation is where 翻译腔 creeps in most.

**Protected spans in translation:** translation into Chinese is the one bounded exception to **Fidelity — protected spans** in `SKILL.md`: render a drug's generic name and a statute's title in their official Chinese form where one exists (`atorvastatin` → `阿托伐他汀`), and write a clause number in the target citation's form without changing its value (`Article 39` → `第三十九条`). Keep identifiers, trademarks, every other number, units, doses, and URLs verbatim.

**Locale and script:** write the vocabulary of the settled locale — mainland `软件`/`数据`/`网络` against Taiwan `軟體`/`資料`/`網路` — and never mix simplified and traditional in one text outside the protected spans. A 简繁 conversion does not run over protected spans.

## Checks

- [ ] The translation reads as Chinese written from the meaning, not clause by clause.
- [ ] Only generic drug names, statute titles and clause-number forms changed in translation; every other protected span is verbatim.
- [ ] Vocabulary and script match the settled locale; no simplified and traditional mixed outside protected spans.
