# Clean Document

Help intended readers find, understand and use what they need. Apply this method across languages and genres whenever drafting, editing or reviewing documents, whether they are the main request or part of a larger task, in files or delivered inline, including instructions executed by agents. Prioritize **authority and fidelity → reader purpose and completeness → structure → expression → brevity**. Honor applicable domain rules, owner requirements and fixed formats; this method does not waive them.

Apply the passes when drafting or editing with authorization. In read-only review, report concrete issues with their location, reader impact and proposed fix; review grants no editing authority. Scale work to the task: a short piece may need only a mental pass, without a process record or output template.

## 1. Establish the reader's task

Identify the reader or executing agent and what they need to know, decide, do or experience. Infer language, genre, voice, scope and delivery constraints from the request, sources and destination. Identify required content, governance, revision histories and protected formats.

Ground factual claims in supplied or authoritative sources; distinguish facts, proposals, assumptions and unknowns. Invent only where authorized, as in fiction. Ask about a gap when it prevents a sound result; otherwise work within the evidence and expose material uncertainty.

## 2. Build a usable structure

Organize around the reader's task and genre. Put prerequisites before dependent actions, definitions where needed, and consequences beside the decisions they qualify. Group related material and give each passage a purpose; fix misplaced explanations before polishing sentences.

Use headings and navigation where they help readers find and follow the content. Let deliberate pacing and revelation serve creative work: ✓ Mystery: “The door was locked from inside. Only later did Mara notice the wet glove.” The withheld explanation creates suspense while the scene remains intelligible.

## 3. Remove content without losing function

Ask what each passage contributes to the reader's understanding, decision, action or intended experience. Remove filler, empty framing, irrelevant detail and repetition without a separate function. Merge overlaps into a coherent owning passage without losing distinct content. Update affected references; leave an integrated document without accidental patch residue.

Judge duplication by function, not matching words. Retain summaries, local warnings, context, worked explanations and refrains when they serve distinct reading paths, safety, learning or artistic effects. Keep required histories and records in their prescribed form and place.

- ✓ Runbook: Repeat “Disconnect power before opening the cover” before each procedure that may be followed independently.
- ✓ Lesson: “Area is length × width. A 3 m by 2 m room covers 6 m². Multiply the two dimensions to find the area.” The recap reinforces the operation.
- ✓ Poem: Repeat “Still, the river runs” as a refrain when its changing context develops the poem's meaning or rhythm.

Treat a change in needed meaning or function as substantive; make it only within the task's authority and disclose it to the requester. Do not disguise a substantive change as shortening.

## 4. Clarify expression and representation

Use natural wording and syntax with the precision, tone and rhythm the language and genre need. Supply concrete actors, actions or relationships only from sources or authorized invention. Keep established terminology consistent and explain unfamiliar terms without paraphrasing exact identifiers.

Simplify overloaded sentences. Choose forms that expose relationships: prose for connected reasoning, steps for ordered actions, tables for comparable attributes where useful. Preserve purposeful voice, imagery and rhythm.

Preserve facts, numbers and units, conditions, exceptions, scope limits, uncertainty, citations and exact identifiers. Preserve normative force: “may,” “should” and “must” carry different permissions and obligations. For executable instructions, preserve triggers, ordering dependencies, branches, tool arguments and completion conditions; cleaner wording must not change what an agent is allowed or required to do. Treat changes to these as substantive changes under pass 3.

## 5. Verify the whole result

Follow the relevant reading or execution path as the intended reader. Check whether they can find what they need, understand relationships and complete the task or receive the intended experience. Recheck transitions and references after structural changes.

Compare edits with their source for lost distinctions, altered claims or force, and unintended shifts of voice; compare drafts with their sources and requirements. Check calculations, identifiers, links, syntax or rendering where affected. Flag unsupported claims or unresolved contradictions rather than inventing a resolution. Deliver in the requested format and report material substantive changes or uncertainties without an unnecessary editorial report.

## Self-check

- **Reader and authority:** Are pass 1's reader task, evidence, uncertainties and applicable constraints reflected in the work, within the authorized mode?
- **Structure:** Does pass 2's organization support the reader's task and genre, with useful forms and dependencies in place?
- **Function:** Did pass 3 distinguish dispensable content from purposeful repetition, preserve required records, integrate passages coherently and handle substantive changes within authority?
- **Expression and fidelity:** Does pass 4's wording fit the language and genre while preserving the required meaning, details, normative force and execution semantics?
- **Verification:** Are pass 5's reading and relevant source or mechanical checks complete, with material changes and unresolved issues reported as required?
